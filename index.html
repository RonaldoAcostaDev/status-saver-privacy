1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Status Saver Android application (package com.composerastudio.satussaver, the “App”), published on Google Play by Compose R.A Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”). It explains what information the App accesses, what third parties may collect through it, and the choices you have.

By installing or using the App you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

2. Data the app uses on your device

Status Saver works entirely on your phone. The information below is processed locally and is never sent to us — we do not operate any server that could receive it.

WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business status folders

When you have viewed a status in WhatsApp, WhatsApp itself stores a copy of that file on your device. To list those statuses, the App asks you to grant access to the status folder through Android's system folder picker (Storage Access Framework). The App can only see the single folder you grant; it does not request “All files access”. You can revoke this access at any time in Settings › Folders inside the App, or from Android's system settings.

Saved photos and videos

When you tap Save, or when you have turned on Auto save, the App copies the selected file to your device's public gallery folders (Pictures/Status Saver and Movies/Status Saver). These copies stay on your device, belong to you, and remain there even if you uninstall the App. You can delete them from the App or from any gallery app.

Sharing and reposting

When you share or repost a file, it is handed only to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). If that app cannot read the file directly, a temporary copy is placed in the App's private cache and is deleted automatically the next time the App starts.

Background checks and notifications (optional)

If you enable Notifications or Auto save, the App periodically checks the granted folder in the background (on your device, about every 15 minutes, subject to Android's battery rules) to detect new statuses, notify you about them, or save them. To avoid repeating alerts, it remembers the file names of statuses it has already seen. Turning both options off stops these checks.

Installed-app check

The App checks whether WhatsApp and/or WhatsApp Business are installed, only to decide which tabs to show. It does not read the list of your other installed apps.

Preferences

Your settings (theme, language, grid size, favourites, notification and auto-save choices) and the folder access grant are stored privately on your device. Folder grants and the list of seen statuses are excluded from Android backups.

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, messages, location or the content of your statuses. The App does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free, it displays full-screen (interstitial) ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). Ads are shown only occasionally after some of your actions, never on the welcome (onboarding) screens.

When ads are requested, Google may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, App version);
  • ad interaction data (for example, which ads were shown and whether they were tapped);
  • diagnostic information related to ad performance.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse, and — only where permitted and where you have agreed if consent is required — to show personalized ads. We do not receive this information in a way that identifies you personally.

4. Consent and your ad choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management tool certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework, to collect and store your privacy choices.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Before personalized ads are shown, you are asked for your consent. You can accept, decline, or choose specific purposes and partners. If you decline, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads, which rely on contextual information rather than your profile.

United States

If you live in a U.S. state whose privacy laws give you the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information or of targeted advertising (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others), the App offers an option to opt out. When you opt out, Google limits the use of your data for personalized advertising.

Changing your choice

Whenever such options apply to you, an Ad privacy entry appears in the App under Settings › Privacy. Open it at any time to review or change your consent or opt-out.

Device-level controls

On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path may vary by device). After deleting the advertising ID, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads.

5. In-app purchases

The App may offer optional purchases (for example, removing ads). All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive your card details or other payment information; the App only receives a confirmation of the purchase status from Google Play so it can unlock the item. Google's handling of payment data is governed by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

7. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share your files with anyone. The only third parties that receive data through the App are Google (AdMob and the User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent, and Google Play for purchases), as described above.

Under some U.S. state laws, allowing an advertising partner to collect data for personalized ads may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. You can opt out as explained in section 4.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, but since we do not hold your personal data, there is generally nothing we could disclose.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device (settings, folder grant, seen-status list, temporary share copies) is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.
  • Saved photos and videos remain in your gallery until you delete them, because they are your files.
  • Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google's retention policies. You can manage or delete Google-held data through your Google account and the controls listed above.

9. Security

Because the App processes your statuses only on your device and does not transmit them, the main protection for your files is the security of your phone itself. App settings are kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Communication between the App and Google's advertising services is encrypted by Google's SDKs. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we design the App to keep data on your device whenever possible.

10. Children's privacy
